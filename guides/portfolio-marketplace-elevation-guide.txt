1. WHAT THIS MARKETPLACE IS AND WHAT STORY IT TELLS
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
The Portfolio Management marketplace is the intelligence and visibility layer
of the DEWA DTMP. It is the EA (Enterprise Architecture) Office's control
tower — a single, continuously updated view of DEWA's entire digital, physical
operational, and data estate. It does not execute delivery. It identifies
where attention is needed and routes that attention into governed action
through Lifecycle Management.

The relationship between Portfolio and Lifecycle is the most important
intersection in the entire platform:
  Portfolio says → "This application is Critical and has no active initiative."
  Lifecycle responds → "We will wrap it in a governed initiative with a framework,
  a template, and TO-monitored delivery."

Portfolio is the diagnostic. Lifecycle is the treatment.

NARRATIVE POSITIONING STATEMENT (keep this in the UI hero copy exactly):
  "A single, continuously updated view of DEWA's entire digital estate —
  applications, infrastructure, operational assets, data platforms, and
  AI models — under one governance lens. Three dimensions of DEWA's estate.
  One control tower."

THE THREE-LENS MODEL (THE CORE CONCEPT OF THE ELEVATED MARKETPLACE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The elevated portfolio marketplace introduces a deliberate three-lens framing
for DEWA's asset estate. This is a client perception decision as much as a
structural one. It signals:

  "We understand that DEWA is not just an IT organisation. Your estate has
   three distinct dimensions — and we have built governance visibility
   across all three."

The three lenses:

  IT ASSETS — The enterprise digital backbone:
    Applications, infrastructure, cloud, integration platforms, security tools.
    These are what divisions use daily. They have health scores, technical debt,
    lifecycle stages, and TCO.

  OT ASSETS — The operational physical estate:
    Control systems, smart meters, IoT sensors, industrial equipment with digital
    interfaces. These are what generates power, distributes water, and keeps the
    lights on. Digitisation % and gap states are the primary metrics.

  DATA & DIGITAL ASSETS — DEWA's future:
    Data platforms, AI/ML models, digital customer products, external APIs.
    This is where DEWA's D-Eleven and AI ambitions live. Treating these as
    governed assets — alongside applications and infrastructure — is what
    no traditional portfolio tool does. This is the forward-looking lens
    that differentiates this platform.

The four governance intelligence tabs remain unchanged:
  - Project Portfolio (what is being delivered)
  - Transformation Initiatives (why it is being delivered)
  - Technology Rationalisation (what needs consolidating)
  - Governance Health (how well the estate is governed)

WHAT THE MARKETPLACE DOES NOT DO
---------------------------------
It does not execute delivery. It does not manage tasks or timelines directly.
It is a read-and-act intelligence surface. Every "act" button (Initiate in
Lifecycle, Request Report) is a hand-off to another marketplace or the TO
office. This distinction must be preserved in every design decision.

3. TAB STRUCTURE — FROM 6 TO 8 (THE THREE-LENS MODEL)
================================================================================

BEFORE (6 tabs, flat):
  1. Application Portfolio
  2. Project Portfolio
  3. Transformation Initiatives
  4. Technology Rationalisation
  5. Governance Health
  6. Operational Asset Digitisation

AFTER (8 tabs, grouped into two visual sections):

  ASSET ESTATE (3 tabs):
  1. IT Assets
  2. OT Assets
  3. Data & Digital

  GOVERNANCE INTELLIGENCE (4 tabs — unchanged in name and content):
  4. Projects
  5. Initiatives
  6. Rationalisation
  7. Governance

NOTE ON TAB LABELS: Use short labels in the tab bar (IT Assets, OT Assets,
Data & Digital, Projects, Initiatives, Rationalisation, Governance). The full
descriptive names appear in the tab content header below the tab bar.

WHY THIS STRUCTURE MATTERS FOR CLIENT PERCEPTION:
  A client such as DEWA — which sees itself as a global benchmark utility and
  a technology leader — will immediately notice that this platform understands
  their estate beyond IT. The presence of OT Assets and Data & Digital as
  first-class governed dimensions communicates that the platform was designed
  for DEWA specifically, not adapted from a generic IT governance tool.
  This structural decision is a statement about how deeply the builder
  understands the client's world.

  The bridge between physical operations and digital governance."

  - Control Systems (SCADA, DCS, PLC networks)
  - Smart Metering (electricity and water AMI networks)
  - IoT & Field Sensors (condition monitoring, environmental, predictive)
  - Industrial Equipment (turbines, transformers, pumps, desalination)
  - Monitoring Infrastructure (substations, CCTV, environmental monitoring)

  Division: dropdown (same divisions)
  Asset Type: checkboxes — all 5 types above

  Asset State: checkboxes — Digitised | Partially Digitised | Not Digitised
  Has Active Initiative: Yes / No / All

7. NEW TAB: DATA & DIGITAL ASSET PORTFOLIO
================================================================================

CONCEPT:
  This tab is entirely new. It governs DEWA's data, AI, and digital product
  assets as first-class governed entities. This is DEWA's most forward-looking
  asset class — the place where D-Eleven ambitions, Virtual Engineer, and
  digital service delivery live.

  No traditional portfolio management tool has this tab. Its presence signals
  that the platform was designed for where DEWA is going, not just where it
  is today.

TAB HEADER COPY:
  Title: "Data & Digital Asset Portfolio"
  Subtitle: "DEWA's data platforms, AI models, digital customer products, and
  external APIs — governed as strategic assets with the same rigour as
  applications and infrastructure. The intelligence and innovation layer
  of DEWA's digital estate."

ASSET TYPES (filter within the tab):
  - Data Platforms (data lake, warehouse, analytics infrastructure)
  - AI & ML Models (deployed models, pipelines, model registries)
  - Digital Customer Products (customer-facing apps, portals, channels)
  - External APIs & Digital Services (developer APIs, partner integrations)

FILTER SIDEBAR:
  Division: dropdown (all divisions)
  Asset Type: checkboxes — all 4 types above
  Status: On Track | At Risk | Critical | No Initiative
  Model/Platform Maturity: Conceptual | Pilot | Production | Scaling | Deprecated
  Has Active Initiative: Yes / No / All
  Clear Filters button

CARD BODY (Data & Digital asset cards):
  All share the universal skeleton (Section 11).
  Body content varies by asset type:

  Data Platform cards:
    Health Score | Platform Type (Data Lake / Warehouse / Analytics / Streaming)
    Data Volume (TB) | Active Pipelines count | Data Quality Score %
    Governed Domains (e.g., "Customer, Financial, Operational")

  AI & ML Model cards:
    Model Status (Production / Pilot / Deprecated)
    Model Accuracy % (or performance metric relevant to the model type)
    Last Retrained date | Training Data Freshness
    Use Case (e.g., "Predictive Maintenance — Generation Turbines")
    Bias/Fairness Review: Passed | Pending | Not Assessed
    Linked OT Asset (if the model operates on operational data)

  Digital Customer Product cards:
    Health Score | Channel Type (Mobile App / Web Portal / EV Platform / Chatbot)
    Monthly Active Users | Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)
    Last Release Date | Accessibility Compliance: Passed / Partial / Failed

  External API & Digital Service cards:
    Health Score | API Type (REST / GraphQL / Streaming)
    Active Consumers (count of systems/partners consuming the API)
    Uptime % (last 30 days) | Deprecation Date (if scheduled)
    Monetisation: Yes / No

marketplace. All card interactions navigate to a full detail page.

  - Remove the "View Insights" button from all card renders
  - Remove the "Initiate in Lifecycle" button from all card renders
  - Keep: card onClick navigating to the detail page

  The InsightsDrawer component file may be kept if used elsewhere, but it
  must not be rendered by PortfolioManagementPage.tsx.

16. CROSS-TAB RELATIONSHIPS ON DETAIL PAGE
================================================================================

CONCEPT:
  The portfolio's six (now eight) tabs represent different views of the same
  estate. An application card is related to a project card (the project
  delivering work on it) which is related to an initiative card (the programme
  it sits under). These relationships exist in the data (projectTag,
  initiativeTag, parentInitiative fields) but are not navigable in the current
  UX. The detail page is where these connections become visible and traversable.

IMPLEMENTATION IN "LINKED ASSETS" TAB:

  For each card, resolve its relationships:

  Application / IT Asset cards:
    - Linked Project: find projectCard where projectCard.name matches card.projectTag
    - Linked Initiative: find initiativeCard where initiativeCard.name matches card.initiativeTag
    - Linked OT Asset: if the application is a control system application,
      find OT cards in the same division

  Project cards:
    - Parent Initiative: find initiativeCard where name matches card.parentInitiative
    - Related Applications: find appCards where projectTag matches card.name
    - Rationalisation items: find rationalisationCards where divisionsAffected
      includes card.division

  Initiative cards:
    - Related Projects: find projectCards where parentInitiative matches card.name
    - Related Applications: find appCards where initiativeTag matches card.name

  OT Asset cards:
    - Linked AI Model: find AI model cards where linkedOTAsset matches card.id
    - Linked Initiative: find initiativeCard where name matches card.initiativeTag
    - Linked Data Platform: find data platform cards in same division

  AI Model cards:
    - Linked OT Asset: resolve card.linkedOTAsset to the OT card
    - Linked Data Platform: find data platform cards in same division
    - Parent Initiative: find initiativeCard where name matches card.initiativeTag

DISPLAY FORMAT (inside "Linked Assets" tab):

  Each relationship group has a label:
    "Linked Projects" | "Parent Initiative" | "Related Applications" |
    "Linked OT Assets" | "AI Models Operating on This Asset" | etc.

  Each linked item renders as a compact relationship card:
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ [small gradient chip by division colour]               │
  │ [Card Name]              [Status badge]                │
  │ [Tab label] · [Division]      [→ View detail] link     │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  "→ View detail" navigates to that card's detail page.
  This allows traversal through the portfolio graph — from an application
  to its project to its initiative to its governance score — without
  returning to the browse grid.

  If no relationships found for a group: don't show the group.
  If no relationships found at all: show a message:
    "No cross-portfolio links found for this asset. If this asset has
    related projects or initiatives, link them by updating the relevant
    portfolio cards."

  Tile 4: EA Compliance Score
    Value: average architectureCompliance across all governance health cards
    Display: as a percentage (e.g. 84%)
    Icon: Shield
    Label: "Avg. EA Compliance"

  Tile 5: Rationalisation Opportunity
    Value: sum of savingPotential across all rationalisation cards
    Display: formatted as "AED X.XM"
    Icon: TrendingUp
    Label: "Rationalisation Savings"

  Full-width horizontal strip, 5-column grid.
  bg-white border border-gray-100 rounded-xl shadow-sm px-4 py-3
  Each tile: flex-col, icon + large number + small label
  Responsive: 3+2 on tablet, 1-column on mobile (scrollable)
  Gap: gap-4

COMPUTED FROM:
  All card arrays: allITCards, allOTCards, dataDigitalCards, projectCards,
  initiativeCards, rationalisationCards, governanceCards.
  Use useMemo() so computation only runs when card data changes.
